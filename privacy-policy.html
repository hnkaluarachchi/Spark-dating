9. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Spark Dating has zero tolerance for any content, behavior, or activity that involves child sexual abuse or exploitation. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Sharing, uploading, or attempting to share any material depicting child sexual abuse.
  • Grooming: Any communication or behavior intended to manipulate or exploit a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion: Threatening or coercing anyone, especially minors, for sexual content or favors.
  • Sexual Exploitation: Any activity that promotes or engages in the sexual exploitation of children.
  • Inappropriate Content: Any content that sexualizes minors or promotes child abuse.

We actively monitor and take immediate action against any such violations, including:

  • Immediate removal of content
  • Permanent account suspension and banning
  • Reporting to relevant law enforcement authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
  • Providing all necessary information to support investigations
